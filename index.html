Astronomical Foundations

Every calendar is an attempt to reconcile three cycles that don't divide evenly into each other: the day, the lunar month, and the solar year.

The Solar Year & the Seasons

Day 0

Earth's elliptical orbit and 23.4° axial tilt produce the equinoxes and solstices that anchor most solar calendars. Drag to move Earth around its orbit.

The Lunar Month

Day 0.0

The synodic month (new moon to new moon) averages 29.53 days — never a whole number, which is why every lunar and lunisolar calendar needs a correction scheme.
